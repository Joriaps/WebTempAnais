What is it?

We apply a special lash and brow tint that darkens the hair and makes it more visible. Your lashes look longer because the light tips are tinted too, and your brows look more defined and even, with no need for make-up.

How it works

What happens in your skin

The tint deposits pigment inside the hair, which is usually lighter at the tips. By evening out the colour from root to tip, the hair is visible along its full real length. The colour gradually fades with washing and as the hair naturally renews.

Benefits

The mascara effect, without mascara

The light tips of your lashes are tinted, so they look longer.

Defined brows

Visually fills in gaps and evens out the colour.

Super quick

You'll have the result in about 20 minutes.

Perfect for summer

Ideal for the beach, the pool or sport, with no make-up to run.

Colour to suit you

We choose the shade that goes with your hair and skin.

Goes with everything

The finishing touch to a lash lift or brow lamination.

Who is it for?

  • People with light or blonde brows or lashes
  • Anyone who wants to go make-up-free and still look defined
  • Holidays and summer
  • As an add-on to a lash lift or brow lamination

What happens in the session?

  1. Choosing the shade

    We pick the colour based on your hair and the effect you're after.

  2. Protecting

    We protect the surrounding skin so it doesn't stain.

  3. Applying

    We apply the tint and leave it to work for a few minutes.

  4. Removing

    We cleanse the area and check the result.

Results

You'll notice it straight away. On brows, the colour lasts around 3-4 weeks, and on lashes up to 6.

Results vary from person to person. We'll explain everything at your initial assessment.

Pairs well with

Treatments that boost or complement the results. We'll help you build your plan at your consultation.

  • Lash liftYour natural lashes, curled and looking longer for 6-8 weeks. No eyelash curler needed.
  • Brow laminationGroomed, tidy, fuller-looking brows for 4-6 weeks. That 'freshly brushed' effect.

Aftercare

  • Avoid getting the area wet for a few hours
  • Don't use oil-based make-up removers for the first few days: they can lighten the shade
  • Protect your brows from the sun so the colour lasts longer
  • Avoid exfoliants and peels on your brows

Contraindications

  • Allergy to tints: we do a sensitivity test 48 h beforehand if it's your first time
  • Eye infections or irritation
  • Wounds or irritation on the area

If you have any doubts about your case, ask us before booking.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an allergy test?

If it's your first time or you have sensitive skin, yes. It's quick and gives us peace of mind.

Can I tint just my brows or just my lashes?

Of course, you can have either one separately or both together.

Can you tell it's a tint?

Not if we choose the right shade. We aim for a natural look.
