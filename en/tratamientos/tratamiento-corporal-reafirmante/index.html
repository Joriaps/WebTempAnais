What is it?

It's a personalised body protocol that combines exfoliation, manual techniques, beauty devices and firming or slimming skincare in a single session. Depending on your goal, we choose from body radiofrequency, cavitation, diathermy, roll systemic, body sculpt, wood therapy or pressotherapy to work on firmness, cellulite or contour, with a plan and progress tracking.

How it works

What happens in your skin

Each technique acts on a different level of the tissue. Heat-based technologies, such as radiofrequency or diathermy, stimulate collagen and elastin in the dermis; cavitation works on localised fat; mechanical techniques and pressotherapy boost microcirculation and lymphatic drainage; and muscle stimulation works on tone. By combining them in the right order, the effects add up and the result is more complete than with a single technique.

Who is it for?

  • Anyone looking for a complete plan who doesn't know where to start
  • Mild or moderate sagging skin or cellulite
  • Getting ready for summer, a wedding or an event
  • After weight changes

What happens in the session?

  1. Diagnosis

    Free initial consultation, measurements and photos to track progress.

  2. Exfoliation

    We prepare the skin so it absorbs the active ingredients better.

  3. Technology or massage

    We apply the right technique for your goal: radiofrequency, cavitation, diathermy, roll systemic, body sculpt or wood therapy.

  4. Wrap or mask

    Firming or slimming products to enhance the effect.

  5. Review

    Every few sessions, we review your measurements and adjust the plan.

Results

The first changes in your skin are usually noticeable after 3-4 sessions. The final result depends on each person and their habits.

Results vary from person to person. We'll explain everything at your initial assessment.

Pairs well with

Treatments that boost or complement the results. We'll help you build your plan at your consultation.

  • Body radiofrequencyDeep heat that stimulates collagen to improve firmness, sagging skin and the appearance of cellulite.
  • Body sculptIntense, effortless muscle stimulation to tone the tummy, buttocks, arms or legs.
  • PressotherapyAir-pressure boots that help drain fluid and leave your legs feeling ultra-light.
  • Brazilian micropuncture for stretch marksA manual technique that stimulates the skin to improve the appearance of stretch marks, including white ones.

Aftercare

  • Moisturise your skin daily with the recommended skincare
  • Drink 1.5-2 litres of water a day, especially on session days
  • Exercise regularly: it boosts and maintains the results
  • Cut down on salt, sugar and alcohol during the plan
  • Avoid strong sun, saunas and very hot baths straight after the session

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Serious circulation problems or thrombosis
  • Pacemaker or metal implants (depending on the technology)
  • Wounds or skin problems on the area
  • Allergy to any of the skincare products in the protocol

If you have any doubts about your case, ask us before booking.

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from beauty devices alone?

Here we combine several techniques and skincare in each session, with a plan and progress tracking.

Can I choose the areas?

Yes, we work on the ones that concern you most.

When will I see results?

Skin usually feels softer early on; changes in firmness or contour take several sessions.
