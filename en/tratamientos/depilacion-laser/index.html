What is it?

A diode laser emits light at a very specific wavelength that is absorbed by melanin, the pigment in hair. That energy turns into heat in the follicle, the structure the hair grows from, and weakens it without damaging the surrounding skin. Session by session, the hair grows back less and less, finer and lighter. It's one of the most widely used methods for long-lasting hair reduction, for both women and men.

How it works

What happens in your skin

The diode light travels through the skin and is absorbed by the pigment in the hair shaft, which carries it down to the follicle. There, the heat weakens the cells responsible for growth, especially when the hair is in its active (anagen) phase. As not all hairs are in that phase at the same time, several spaced-out sessions are needed to reach them all. The cooled handpiece protects the outer layer of the skin and makes the session more comfortable.

Areas and packs for women and men.

Who is it for?

  • Anyone tired of removing hair every few days
  • People with ingrown hairs or irritation after shaving
  • Women and men with dark or brown hair (where it works best)
  • Over-16s (under-16s with consent from a parent or guardian)

What happens in the session?

  1. Free initial consultation

    We assess your skin and hair, do a patch test and you sign the consent form.

  2. Preparation

    You come with the area shaved (with a razor, never waxed) and no creams.

  3. Session

    We pass the handpiece over the area. You'll feel cold and slight warmth or a little prick.

  4. Aftercare

    We apply a soothing cream and give you advice for the following days.

Results

Many people notice less hair from the first few sessions. The final result depends on hair type, skin and hormonal factors, so it varies from person to person.

Results vary from person to person. We'll explain everything at your initial assessment.

Pairs well with

Treatments that boost or complement the results. We'll help you build your plan at your consultation.

  • WaxingSmooth skin straight away and for several weeks. Warm or hot wax depending on the area.
  • Deep cleansing facialA thorough cleanse, extraction of impurities and fresh, radiant skin from day one.

Aftercare

  • For 4 days before and 4 days after each session, avoid direct sun and don't get sunburnt. You can carry on completely as normal; just avoid strong direct sun exposure
  • Use SPF 50 sunscreen on exposed areas
  • Don't wax or tweeze between sessions; only shave or use hair removal cream
  • Avoid saunas, swimming pools and intense exercise for 24-48 h
  • Moisturise the area with a soothing cream or aloe vera
  • Avoid alcohol-based deodorants, perfumes and exfoliants on the area for 48 h
  • It's normal to see hairs "coming out" over the following days: they're shedding, so don't pull them out

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Strong direct sun exposure or sunburn in the 4 days before the session
  • Photosensitising medication (some antibiotics, isotretinoin, etc.)
  • Wounds, infections or active lesions on the area
  • Tattoos on the area to be treated
  • Photosensitive epilepsy or active skin conditions: check with us first

If you have any doubts about your case, ask us before booking.

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as a little prick or an elastic band snap with warmth. The handpiece cools the skin and it's quite bearable.

Does it work on blonde, red or grey hair?

The laser targets dark pigment, so on very light or white hair the results are very limited. We'll be honest with you at the consultation.

Is it permanent?

We talk about long-lasting reduction: hair is greatly reduced, but hormonal changes can cause some new growth, so you may need the odd top-up.

Can I do it in summer?

Yes, on areas not exposed to the sun and with sun protection. For exposed areas, we assess each case.

Why do I need to shave beforehand?

So the light reaches the root and doesn't burn the hair on the surface. Waxing would pull out the root and the laser would have nothing to work on.
