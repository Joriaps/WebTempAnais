What is it?

Body sculpt is a body contouring treatment using high-intensity electromagnetic technology. It triggers very intense, repeated muscle contractions without you having to make any effort or have surgery. It helps tone and firm the tummy, buttocks, arms or thighs, and it's a great complement to exercise and to treatments for sagging skin and localised fat.

How it works

What happens in your skin

The applicator creates an electromagnetic field that passes through the skin and fat and reaches the muscle. There it triggers very intense contractions, known as supramaximal contractions, that can't be achieved with voluntary exercise, and far more repetitions than you'd manage in the same time. The muscle responds by adapting: it gains tone and firmness, just as after a workout. This work also boosts the metabolism of the area; some studies have also seen a moderate reduction in the fat covering the muscle, although results vary a lot from person to person.

Who is it for?

  • People at a stable weight who want to tone specific areas
  • A tummy lacking tone after pregnancy (once medically signed off)
  • Buttocks, arms or legs lacking firmness
  • Anyone who trains and wants extra definition

What happens in the session?

  1. Free initial consultation

    We assess your areas, goal and any contraindications.

  2. Positioning

    We secure the applicator over the area with a strap.

  3. Session

    The device generates intense contractions; we adjust the intensity to what you can comfortably take.

  4. Finish

    We remove the applicator and you can carry on with your day.

Results

You'll feel the area has been worked from the first session. Improvements in tone and definition are gradual and keep progressing over the weeks after the course. Results vary depending on your body and habits.

Results vary from person to person. We'll explain everything at your initial assessment.

Pairs well with

Treatments that boost or complement the results. We'll help you build your plan at your consultation.

  • Body radiofrequencyDeep heat that stimulates collagen to improve firmness, sagging skin and the appearance of cellulite.
  • CavitationLow-frequency ultrasound that helps reduce localised fat in specific areas.
  • PressotherapyAir-pressure boots that help drain fluid and leave your legs feeling ultra-light.
  • Diathermy (body and face)Deep heat generated from within the tissue to firm, boost circulation and improve cellulite. Also available for the face.

Aftercare

  • Drink water after the session
  • It's normal to feel slightly stiff the next day
  • Follow a balanced, protein-rich diet
  • Combine with physical activity to boost and maintain the results
  • Leave 48-72 hours of rest between sessions on the same area

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Pacemaker, defibrillator or other implanted electronic devices
  • Metal implants or prostheses in the area, copper IUD (tummy)
  • Epilepsy
  • Abdominal hernias or recent surgery on the area
  • Active cancer, fever or heart disease
  • Recent muscle injuries in the area

If you have any doubts about your case, ask us before booking.

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace the gym?

No. It's a complement that helps tone specific areas; exercise and diet are still the foundation.

What does it feel like?

Intense muscle contractions, like a very concentrated workout. We adjust the intensity throughout.

Can I have several areas treated?

Yes, in the same appointment or on alternate days.

Does it get rid of fat?

It isn't a weight-loss treatment: its main job is to tone the muscle. It can help improve your contour and, if you want to work on localised fat, we combine it with cavitation.
