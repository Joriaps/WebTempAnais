Your welcome to Imperium

Aloha

Is it your first time with us? We'll welcome you with 15% off your first treatment and a free assessment so you know exactly what's right for you before you pay a thing.

  • 15% off your first treatment (anything on the menu)
  • Free skin or area assessment before you start
  • Free patch test for laser, Dermapen and micropuncture
  • We'll give you the price and number of sessions in writing, no obligation

Terms: Only for first-time clients. One use per person, on a single treatment. Cannot be combined with other offers or applied to packs or packages that are already discounted. Under-18s need permission from a parent or guardian.

Bring your people, you both win

Ohana

At Imperium we're family. If you bring a friend, partner or family member who's new to us, you both get 20% off: your guest on their first visit and you on your next appointment.

  • 20% off for your friend or family member on their first treatment
  • 20% off for you on your next treatment
  • No limit: every new person you bring means another 20% for you
  • You can come together on the same day and share a treatment room if available

Terms: Your guest must be a new client and give your name when booking. Your 20% is activated once they complete their first appointment and expires after 3 months. One discount per treatment; cannot be combined with Aloha or other offers, or applied to packs or packages.
